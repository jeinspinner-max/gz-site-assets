### What is the best DTF printer in Australia?

### DTF vs sublimation - which should I buy?
Choose DTF for multi-fabric printing (cotton, polyester, blends, dark fabrics) and small-batch garments. Choose sublimation for high-volume polyester printing (sportswear, jerseys, flags). DTF offers more versatility; sublimation offers faster production on polyester.

### What can you print with a UV flatbed printer?
UV flatbed printers print directly onto: acrylic, wood, glass, metal, ceramic tiles, leather, PVC, foam board, phone cases, tumblers (with rotary attachment), signage, packaging, and promotional products. UV ink cures instantly for scratch-resistant, waterproof output.

### What is the difference between UV DTF and regular DTF?
Regular DTF uses heat transfer for textiles (t-shirts, fabrics, garments). UV DTF uses cold-apply adhesive for hard surfaces (phone cases, tumblers, packaging, labels, stickers). They serve completely different markets and are not interchangeable.

### What software do GZ Australia printers use?

### What is UV DTF printing and how does it work?

UV DTF (Direct Transfer Film) prints UV ink onto an A film, then a B laminating film is heat-pressed on top. Unlike heat-transfer DTF for textiles, UV DTF is scratch-resistant, waterproof, and rated for outdoor use.
