### What is the best UV flatbed printer for small business in Australia?
For small businesses, the A3HD ($14,990) handles phone cases, promotional items, and small signage with a 300x420mm print area. For medium production, the H6090EI ($29,990) offers 600x900mm with 3 Epson i3200 heads. Both include everything needed to start printing.

### What is the best DTF printer in Australia?
The Pixy T2 ($26,990) is ideal for startups - a complete DTF system with printer, shaker, and oven. For production-level output, the Pixy T4 ($28,990) adds 4 Epson i3200-A1 heads for 30 sqm/h throughput. Both print on any fabric colour including dark garments.

### DTF vs sublimation - which should I buy?
Choose DTF for multi-fabric printing (cotton, polyester, blends, dark fabrics) and small-batch garments. Choose sublimation for high-volume polyester printing (sportswear, jerseys, flags). DTF offers more versatility; sublimation offers faster production on polyester.

### What can you print with a UV flatbed printer?
UV flatbed printers print directly onto: acrylic, wood, glass, metal, ceramic tiles, leather, PVC, foam board, phone cases, tumblers (with rotary attachment), signage, packaging, and promotional products. UV ink cures instantly for scratch-resistant, waterproof output.

### What is the difference between UV DTF and regular DTF?
Regular DTF uses heat transfer for textiles (t-shirts, fabrics, garments). UV DTF uses cold-apply adhesive for hard surfaces (phone cases, tumblers, packaging, labels, stickers). They serve completely different markets and are not interchangeable.

### What software do GZ Australia printers use?
DTF printers use Onyx DTF RIP software. UV flatbed and UV RTR printers use FlexiPRINT UV or Onyx RIPCenter. Eco-solvent printers use FlexiPRINT or Onyx. All RIP software licences are included with the printer purchase.

Choose by print area: A3HD (300x420mm) for small items, H6090 series (600x900mm) for mid-size, H1612 (1600x1200mm) for large rigid substrates, H2513/H3220 (2.5m+) for industrial-scale production. Epson i3200 heads suit most applications; upgrade to Ricoh Gen6 for maximum industrial throughput.
